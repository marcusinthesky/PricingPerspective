4.2 Finite interaction and the strong-interaction boundary

Let \(P=\mathbf1\pi ^\top \) and \(N=W-P\). When \(P\) is an absorbing idempotent projection, the normalized Leontief multiplier has the exact complementary-resolvent remainder used in the next result.

If \(\lVert \rho (W-P)\rVert {\lt}1\), then

\[ \left\lVert (1-\rho )(I-\rho W)^{-1}-P\right\rVert \leq |1-\rho |\{ 1-\lVert \rho (W-P)\rVert \} ^{-1}\lVert I-P\rVert . \]
Proof ▼

Split the multiplier into the Perron projection and complementary resolvent, then apply the Neumann-series norm bound to the latter.

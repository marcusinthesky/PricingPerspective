## Abstract

Spatial return models usually begin with an interaction matrix supplied by the researcher. This paper constructs a bandwidth-free field from firms' language-model article-embedding distributions. For each target firm, quadratic transport aligns its article cloud separately with every candidate peer. Holding those target-specific correspondences fixed, a simplex problem chooses nonnegative weights that jointly reconstruct the target.

Repeating this reconstruction produces a directed barycentric interaction field. A quadratic exposure-adjustment problem maps spatial feedback into the penalty on peer misalignment relative to departure from stand-alone exposure.

## Reading one peer basket

AMD's five largest reconstruction weights are NVDA (0.1225), INTC (0.0987), MU (0.0878), AMAT (0.0840), and ASML (0.0688). Together they carry about 0.462 of the unit row. The basket spans chip design, memory and equipment supply; the rest of the mass is distributed across additional peers.

A large weight records usefulness within a jointly reconstructing combination. It is neither a portfolio holding nor evidence of causal influence. A firm that is relatively far from the target in pairwise distance can still help reconstruct one part of its information distribution.

## Mathematical sequence

1. Represent each firm as an equally weighted empirical distribution of article embeddings.
2. Solve a target-to-peer optimal-transport assignment for each ordered pair.
3. Freeze those correspondences and reconstruct each target with common simplex weights.
4. Stack the rows into a nonnegative, row-stochastic, zero-diagonal operator.
5. Derive the exposure equation $B = \rho WB + (1 - \rho)\xi$, where $\rho = \lambda/(1+\lambda)$.

## Financial evaluation

The empirical design freezes information fields constructed from 2018–2022 news, estimates spatial factor models, and evaluates conditional return dependence in 2023–2026. The comparisons include pairwise-distance kernels, equal-support fields and persistent news co-mentions. Return resampling conditions on the constructed text geometry.

<Callout title="Boundary">
The spatial coefficient is a conditional working-model quantity. Its adjustment interpretation requires the paper's exposure and return bridges. Freezing the field before the evaluation window does not identify causal peer effects.
</Callout>

\section*{Data availability statement}

The corporate articles come from an archive of Nasdaq financial news, and daily
returns come from commercial and public market-data providers.
The source articles remain subject to their providers' terms and are not
redistributed. The replication package includes computed embeddings,
adjusted-return panels and text-free numerical controls, together with versioned
code, environment specifications and scripts that regenerate the manuscript's
tables and figures.

\section{Data construction and coverage}\label{sec:data-provenance}

The application uses a declared Nasdaq-100-based frame rather than a
reconstructed historical-membership panel. A firm enters the news frame only
if the raw Nasdaq per-symbol archive contains at least
\ppvalue{annual-raw-article-floor} articles in every
calendar year from \ppvalue{sample-year-start} through
\ppvalue{sample-year-end}. The screen ensures annual support for each empirical
article distribution but conditions the sample on media coverage.

The comparison in \Cref{tab:news-dataset-comparison} highlights the trade-off:
the public source permits researcher-controlled full-text measurement but
offers narrower market coverage than large proprietary archives.

Identifier-level deduplication removes
\ppnum[0]{corpus-duplicates-dropped} repeated records. After the fixed window
and deterministic cap, full article bodies are embedded independently with the
pinned encoder and row-normalized before exact balanced W$_2$ construction.
Daily simple returns are adjusted-close ratios minus one; nullable observations
are removed before matrix construction, with no interpolation or zero filling.
The news frame contains \ppnum[0]{n-firms-news} firms, and its intersection with
compatible local return histories retains \ppnum[0]{n-firms} priced firms.

The product-market comparison uses the Hoberg--Phillips ETNIC-All pair
scores, a preliminary release keyed by Compustat identifier, for fiscal years
\ppvalue{etnic-year-start}--\ppvalue{etnic-year-end}
\citep{hoberg_phillips_2016,hoberg2025scope}.
The preliminary files were downloaded on 23 September 2026; the frozen
sample extract and matching crosswalk are preserved with checksums in the
replication materials.
A curated ticker--CIK--Compustat crosswalk links the roster to the network.
Foreign issuers filing annual reports on Form 20-F are excluded by the
network's construction, firms without a crosswalk link are omitted, and firms
without co-observed pairs are dropped, leaving \ppnum[0]{etnic-n-firms} firms
whose every pair is scored.
ETNIC trains a separate doc2vec model on each year's business descriptions
and scores each pair by the cosine similarity of its boilerplate-purged
representations.
Pair similarity here is the mean of those scores over co-observed fiscal
years.
The fiscal-year average overlaps the return window and serves as a
retrospective product-market control; filing dates are not used for alignment.

The headline encoder is \texttt{qwen/qwen3-embedding-8b}, a fixed
semantic-retrieval model with no documented
training cutoff.
The later-window comparison uses articles dated before the evaluation
returns and embeddings computed retrospectively with that model.

The sample correlation underlying the chord distance in
equation~\ref{eq:chord-distance} is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:sample-correlation}
  \hat{\rho}_{ij}
  =
  \frac{
    \sum_{t=1}^{N_T} (r_t^i - \bar{r}^i)(r_t^j - \bar{r}^j)
  }{
    \sqrt{
      \sum_{t=1}^{N_T} (r_t^i - \bar{r}^i)^2
      \sum_{t=1}^{N_T} (r_t^j - \bar{r}^j)^2
    }
  },
\end{equation}
where $N_T$ is the number of common return dates and $\bar{r}^i$ is the
sample average of asset $i$'s returns, yielding a
$\ppvalue{n-firms} \times \ppvalue{n-firms}$ symmetric chord distance matrix
$D$ with zero diagonal.

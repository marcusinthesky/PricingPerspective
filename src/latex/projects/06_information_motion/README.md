# Information Games: Strategic Crowding and Firm Repositioning in Language-Model Space

The paper follows competition for opportunities through costly operating adjustment,
financial repricing, reported information distributions and a frozen-reference directional
estimator. The two-opportunity sign mechanism is sufficient under explicit local economic,
reporting and news assumptions; general multi-activity systems have no universal sign.
The empirical target is the realized historical directional association and conditional
article-measurement sensitivity, without structural identification or population coverage.

## Reproduction

From the repository root:

```bash
dvc repro render@06_information_motion-arxiv render_management_science render_wfbs
```

The historical fixed study retains durable oracle arrays, replicate coefficients, premise checks,
frozen inputs, Semflow code identity and checkpoint hashes under
`data/papers/paper6/information_recovery/campaign`.
The campaign enumerates all sixteen permanent-news histories with 128 article redraws per
history, common and changing reporting, and observed/fourfold/sixteenfold coverage.
The bounded dimension check remains appendix support. Failed premises and undefined
estimates remain explicit; no parameter or seed search changes the recovery result.
The campaign's read-only audit checks its frozen inputs, historical source archives
when present, and checkpoint bytes. Proof certification runs in the separate Lean
DVC stages; numerical resume compatibility uses the existing Semflow identity.

The completed study is an immutable input owned by `information_recovery.dvc`.
`p6_directional_validation` derives its directional diagnostics and runs the
predeclared stationary-law, reporting-only and bounded shock controls in a new
namespace. Its saved inference decision separates iid-history candidate
calibration from conditional article sensitivity.
The main manuscript presents this 160-redraw-per-history study; the appendix
preserves the historical 128-redraw record. A separate 2,500-panel-per-target
iid-history fixture assesses the candidate one-sided bound. Its failed
finite-sample calibration and the archive's different sampling target leave
corporate uncertainty descriptive.

`p6_corporate_analysis` maintains a raw replay of all four retained corporate
cells and 199 paired redraws. The primary `p6_corporate_analysis_render` stage
reads only its saved results; `p6_information_audit_render` preserves the
secondary T82/T83 observation, held-out and locality checks. The simulation-only
`p6_information_recovery_render` projects saved legacy and control summaries.
Each renderer owns separate scalar files and exhibits. No renderer estimates
coefficients or chooses scenarios.

Generated values and figures are producer-owned. Frozen-array reconciliation
precedes display rounding; raw transport uses its solver certificates.
Article-selection quantiles retain their conditional interpretation, including
the genuine fifth percentile. No embeddings or returns are refetched.

`p6_article_redraw_validation` nests the corporate article-redraw procedure inside
known-law generated archives, while `p6_inference_audit` adds the unchanged
candidate's 100-firm iid comparison. Their independent `_render` stages publish
saved summaries and exclusive scalar bindings. Completed historical results and
the complete corporate projection retain their original identities.

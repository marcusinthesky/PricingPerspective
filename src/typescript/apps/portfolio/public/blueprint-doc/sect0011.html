3.3 Reconciling the two geometries

Definition 29 Squared energy functional
#

For laws with finite first distance moments, let

\[ \mathcal E^2(\mu ,\nu ) =2\mathbb E d(X,Y)-\mathbb E d(X,X')-\mathbb E d(Y,Y'), \]

where the four draws are independent with the indicated marginal laws. Negative type ensures nonnegativity; the upper bound below does not require that additional geometric property.

Theorem 30 Wasserstein balls sit inside energy balls
#

Uses

  • Definition 1
  • Definition 29
  • Definition 29

For probability laws with finite first distance moments, if \(W_2(\mu ,\nu )\le \delta \) then \(\mathcal E^2(\mu ,\nu )\le 2\delta \); the Wasserstein ball of radius \(\delta \) is therefore contained in the squared-energy sublevel set with threshold \(2\delta \). A guarantee uniform over this larger sublevel set also holds over the Wasserstein ball; the inclusion does not give the reverse implication. This node belongs with the trunk rather than with this chapter.

Proof ▼

The triangle inequality bounds the energy functional by twice the expected distance under any coupling. Cauchy–Schwarz bounds that expectation by the root mean squared coupling cost; taking the infimum gives the Wasserstein bound.

# Wasserstein Information Matrix

## Summary

Li and Zhao develop Wasserstein analogues of score functions, information
matrices, and covariance operators, extending information-geometric
statistical theory to the Wasserstein metric setting.

## Key findings

### F1 — Wasserstein covariance operators arise in a statistical inference context

- Claim: Transport geometry induces Wasserstein score functions and
  covariance operators that serve as analogues of Fisher scores and
  information matrices for statistical models on metric spaces.
- Evidence: The paper derives Wasserstein-Cramer-Rao bounds and demonstrates
  that transport-induced covariance operators govern efficiency in the
  Wasserstein statistical setting.
- Scope: The covariance objects concern statistical efficiency for parametric
  models, not systematic return covariance between economic agents.

## Assessment

Cite for the overlap in vocabulary: Li and Zhao study covariance operators
in the Wasserstein information-geometric context, while Paper 1 derives
systematic return covariance restrictions from Wasserstein distances between
distribution-valued firm characteristics. The shared language makes the
distinction useful to state explicitly.

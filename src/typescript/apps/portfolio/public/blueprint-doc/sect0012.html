4.1 Operator and convergence certificate

Theorem 30 SAR compatibility of the Wasserstein operator
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • PricingPerspective.Connections.SpatialWasserstein.anchoredW2_sar_gate

A leave-one-out simplex operator is nonnegative, row stochastic, and zero-diagonal. Its induced infinity norm is at most one, so \(\lVert \rho W\rVert _\infty {\lt}1\) whenever \(|\rho |{\lt}1\).

Proof ▼

The simplex constraints give the row sums and nonnegativity; the norm gate follows from the maximum absolute row sum.

Definition 31 Geometric Perron certificate
✓
#

Lean declarations

  • PricingPerspective.Connections.SpatialAsymptotics.HasGeometricPerronBound

For \(P=\mathbf1\pi ^\top \), a geometric Perron certificate records constants \(C\geq 0\) and \(0\leq q{\lt}1\) such that \(\lVert W^k-P\rVert \leq Cq^k\) for every natural number \(k\).
